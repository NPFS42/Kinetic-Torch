S.T.A.R.S. Kinetic Torch lets you toggle your phone's rear LED flashlight with a quick double-chop gesture.

Adjust gesture sensitivity and timing to suit your movements. The app uses the hardware camera flash LED; a device with a flash and accelerometer is required.

Features:
• Double-chop LED flashlight control
• Adjustable gesture sensitivity and timing
• Quick Settings tile
• Automatic shutoff timer
• Proximity guard to help prevent activation while covered
• Vibration confirmation for successful toggles

When armed, Kinetic Torch listens for motion through a foreground service with a persistent notification and a clear disarm control. Continuous listening can increase battery use.

Works offline. No account or ads.
